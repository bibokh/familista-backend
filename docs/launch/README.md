# Familista Design Studio — launch readiness (M8)

Public launch is **NO-GO** (see `docs/design-studio/COMMERCIAL-PROGRAM.md`, section 5). This folder prepares for it
without claiming it: a milestone may be released to invited clubs, behind its rollout control, when the Owner approves.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `claims.json` | Every statement launch material may make, with its evidence. `tests/launch-claims.unit.test.ts` fails if a claim's evidence disappears, or copy cites an unknown or unverified claim. |
| `ROLLOUT.md` | How the Owner releases the studio, and each of its parts, to an invited club; what to check first. |
| `FAQ.md` | Questions clubs ask, answered from verified claims only. |
| `INVITATIONS.md` | Invitation templates for clubs. **Not sent**: nothing here contacts anyone. |
| `legal/PRIVACY-DRAFT.md` | What the studio processes, where it goes and how long it stays: a working draft **for qualified review**. |
| `legal/TERMS-DRAFT.md` | Terms for clubs using the studio and the owner-brokered quotations: a working draft **for qualified review**. |

Nothing in `legal/` is legal advice, and nothing in it is in force. Placeholders in square brackets are for the Owner
and counsel to fill.
