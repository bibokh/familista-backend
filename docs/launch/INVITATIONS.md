## Before sending (the Owner's checklist)

- The club exists on the platform, and its administrator has an account.
- The studio is released to the club (`ROLLOUT.md`), and the parts it should have are on.
- The privacy and terms drafts have been reviewed by qualified counsel, and the club has the versions in force.
- The invitation's wording still cites only verified claims.
