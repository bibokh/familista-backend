# Club Design Studio — questions clubs ask

Each answer states only what the platform verifiably does today (`claims.json`). Where something is not available, the
answer says so.

**What can we design?**
Match shirts, goalkeeper shirts, training tops, T-shirts, shorts, goalkeeper shorts and socks, seen in 2D, 2.5D and 3D.
<!-- claim:studio-garments --> Jackets, hoodies, tracksuits and polos are not available yet.

**Who can see our designs?**
Your club alone. Other clubs cannot list or open them, and the database itself enforces that, not just the screens.
<!-- claim:club-isolation -->

**Can we go back to an earlier version?**
Yes. Every save is kept as it was saved, and any version can be restored as a new one. <!-- claim:design-versions -->

**Can we use our crest and sponsors?**
Yes. Upload them to your brand kit with your club colours. Logos are re-encoded by the server before they are kept.
<!-- claim:brand-kit -->

**Can we add players' names and numbers?**
Yes, for the whole squad: type them or import a spreadsheet, and preview any player's name and number on the garment.
<!-- claim:team-kit --> A collection then counts the sets you need per size. <!-- claim:order-sheet -->

**Who approves a design?**
Anyone on your design team can comment. Only a club administrator approves a version, and an approval cannot be
changed afterwards: a new version needs its own approval. <!-- claim:reviews -->

**Can we show the kit to our board or sponsor?**
Yes. A collection prints, or saves as a PDF, as a presentation of the kit. <!-- claim:presentation -->

**Can the studio suggest an away or third kit?**
It suggests colourways from your design and your club colours, keeping every name, number and mark readable.
<!-- claim:colourways --> It does not use AI.

**How do we get our kit made?**
Ask Familista for a quotation from a collection. Familista obtains quotations from suppliers it has verified and
publishes them to you. Nothing is ordered until you accept one. <!-- claim:quotations-brokered --> After that, you
follow the order through production, shipping and delivery. <!-- claim:order-tracking -->

**Can we pay online?**
Not yet. Nothing is charged through the platform today; payment is agreed separately when an order is confirmed.

**Will the colours be exactly as on screen?**
No screen can promise that. Screen colours are previews; the printed colour depends on the fabric and the process.

**Which languages?**
English, German, French, Spanish and Arabic. <!-- claim:languages -->
