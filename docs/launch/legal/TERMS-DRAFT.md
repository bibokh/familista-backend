# Club Design Studio and owner-brokered manufacturing — terms: DRAFT FOR QUALIFIED REVIEW

> **Not legal advice and not in force.** Headings and points for counsel, from what the platform does today. Square
> brackets are for the Owner and counsel.

## 1. The service

- Familista provides the Club Design Studio to clubs it has released it to. Features available to a club depend on its
  plan and on what Familista has released to it; Familista may change them [notice period].
- Screen colours are previews; the printed colour depends on fabric and process, settled by physical samples.
- Online payment is not provided. Nothing is charged through the platform.

## 2. The club's content

- The club keeps the rights it has in its designs, crests, sponsor marks and colours. It confirms it may use every mark
  it uploads (its own, or with the owner's permission) and grants Familista the licence needed to store, display and,
  when the club asks for a quotation, share them with suppliers [scope].
- Familista re-encodes uploaded logos and refuses some files; it is not responsible for marks the club had no right to use.
- Design templates, the editor and its 3D views remain Familista's [and its licensors'].

## 3. Quotations and orders (owner-brokered)

- When a club asks for a quotation, Familista seeks quotations from suppliers it has verified and publishes them to the
  club. Familista states what it checked about a supplier; it does not [warrant the supplier's work — to decide].
- A quotation is valid until the date shown. Nothing is ordered until the club accepts one **and** Familista confirms the
  order with the supplier.
- **Contract structure to decide:** is the contract for the garments between the club and the supplier, with Familista
  as broker, or between the club and Familista, with Familista buying from the supplier? This decides liability,
  invoicing, VAT, consumer law (if a club is not a business), returns and defects. [Decision for the Owner with counsel.]
- Delivery: the club confirms delivery in the studio; [defects, returns and claims procedure].

## 4. Accounts, approvals and records

- A club administrator's approval of a design version is recorded with its time and the version's fingerprint, and
  cannot be changed afterwards. [Whether an approval binds the club in an order: to decide.]
- Familista keeps an audit record of actions taken in the studio and in manufacturing.
